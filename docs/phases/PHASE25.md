# AquaGuard Phase 25 — Multimodal Risk Fusion & End-to-End Validation

Phase 25 combines validated AI signals with GIS/operational context into a transparent risk-fusion layer.

Signals:
- XGBoost flood probability
- YOLO vision detections
- rainfall intensity
- water level
- drainage/blockage state
- incident history
- GIS zone context

The fusion layer is an explainable development baseline. It must be calibrated and validated against local historical events before production use.
