# AquaGuard — 4 Minute SIH Demo

**0:00–0:35 — Problem.** Urban flooding occurs where intense rainfall, low drainage capacity, blocked assets, vulnerable roads and delayed response interact. AquaGuard fuses those signals into one city operating picture.

**0:35–1:25 — Command Center.** Show the live map, HydroFusion risk pulse, rainfall nowcast, blocked-drain and road KPIs, decision log and crew readiness. Emphasize that AquaGuard converts signals into prioritized decisions, not just charts.

**1:25–2:05 — Hydro Intelligence.** Show the 12-hour rainfall curve, catchments expected to cross thresholds first, drainage inflow versus remaining capacity, and explainable AI factors.

**2:05–2:40 — Satellite Intelligence.** Show the Sentinel workflow: scene discovery → cloud filtering → water extraction → GeoJSON flood extent → PostGIS → road/drain risk fusion.

**2:40–3:20 — Safe Routing.** Explain that AquaGuard's PostGIS router penalizes flood zones, blocked drains and flooded roads. Google Routes can add a traffic-aware baseline; flood safety remains AquaGuard's additional layer.

**3:20–4:00 — Integration Hub.** Show Open-Meteo, Google Maps Platform, Sentinel Hub, PostGIS and FastAPI AI. Close with the modularity story: development feeds can be replaced with authorized city sources without rewriting the frontend.
